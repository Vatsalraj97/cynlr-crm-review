Deal stages represent the post-meeting lifecycle: requirements exchange, POC, proposal, negotiation, close. Campaigns and Deals are sequential, not parallel.

CAMPAIGNS EXIST TO BOOK ONE MEETING. THAT'S IT.
A campaign is a targeted email outreach effort sent to a segmented list of contacts. Goal: get a meeting booked. Once the meeting is confirmed, the campaign's job is done and a Deal is created in HubSpot. All the email-back-and-forth in between — replies, follow-ups, re-scheduling — is campaign activity, not deal activity. Campaigns live entirely in the local CRM. HubSpot only receives a trace: the campaign name gets stamped on the resulting deal and contact.
